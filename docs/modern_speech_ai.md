# Modern Speech AI & SOTA Research (2025-2026)

## The Shift to Empathic Interface (EVI)
Standard speech synthesis is no longer the frontier. The focus has shifted to **Empathic Voice Interfaces (EVI)**.
- **Emotional Prosody**: The ability to understand and replicate fine-grained emotional nuances (stress, pitch, rhythm).
- **Interruption Handling**: SOTA systems (like Hume EVI) handle turn-taking and interruptions naturally using LLM-based endpointing.

## Key SOTA Models
- **Hume EVI (Empathic Voice Interface)**: Built on the "Empathic Large Language Model" (eLLM), it optimizes for human well-being and emotional alignment.
- **ElevenLabs Turbo v2.5**: Optimized for ultra-low latency (<200ms) while maintaining high-fidelity emotional range.
- **GPT-4o / Gemini 3 Pro**: Native multimodal models that reason across audio and text without a separate STT/TTS pipeline.

## Chinese AI Hyperactivity (SOTA 2026)
Research from China has introduced highly efficient, lightweight models:
- **SenseVoice (Alibaba)**: Extremely fast multimodal understanding.
- **ChatTTS**: Optimized for natural conversational dialogue, including filler words like "um" and "uh".
- **GPT-SoVITS**: A powerful zero-shot voice cloning framework that runs efficiently on consumer hardware.

## Dialogic Patterns
- **Backchanneling**: Providing vocal cues ("uh-huh", "right") while the user is speaking to indicate active listening.
- **Prosodic Matching**: The AI adjusts its emotional tone to match or complement the user's state.
